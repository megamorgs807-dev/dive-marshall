About the club

Formed in 1975, still going strong

Bude Dive Club is the 715th branch of the British Sub-Aqua Club — the UK's governing body for recreational scuba diving. We run a full diving calendar for members across Cornwall and Devon, alongside BSAC-certified instructor training.

Our members range from 14 to 75 years old, and we take real pride in giving everyone — from complete beginners to experienced divers — the chance to get in the water and explore the Southwest's coastline.

About us

Membership & training

👥

Membership requirements

The minimum age to start scuba training is 14 (with a parent also training alongside). You'll need to be able to swim two pool lengths freestyle and complete a self-certification medical declaration.

📘

Training

Training follows the BSAC syllabus. Most members reach Open Water standard in around three months — our instructors aren't under commercial pressure, so training moves at the pace you need.

🧭

Already qualified?

Coming from BSAC or PADI? Your existing grade carries across, and if you haven't dived in a while, you'll simply join with a refresher session.
